## Decisions

Report measured candidates as adopted, tested without a qualified gain, or
execution unavailable with its concrete blocker. Host-only results do not
establish console speed. Approximate routing/output quantization disclose
quality changes; exact implementations must preserve complete finite logits,
ordered routes and emitted tokens in their matching scope.

Promote only an actually faster complete workload with all original physical
memory, owner and positive-completion checks. An inconclusive small difference
does not justify unchanged repeated experiments. Qualify the selected combination
in the normal application, view its actual information-only frames, preserve the
rollback, verify installed bytes/settings and finish with ordinary close.

The original startup and memory observations remain current-case requirements;
an application ledger adjustment alone is not reclaimed physical memory.
