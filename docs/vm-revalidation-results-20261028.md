## Current reader versus refill

Case 3419 used the actual production `PairedSourceIO` against a six-demand
completion-refill owner. Both used the caller and one active worker, the same
original extents and destination bytes. The inactive other owner remained
resident in both arms. Initialization was outside the measured interval.
The sealed source pack was reused; native full SHA256, stable identity and EOF
were checked without reuploading or copying its full payload over the network.

All **60 batches / 360 original matrix reads** completed exactly, followed by
positive worker joins and Direct retirement. The median paired-round speed
ratio was **1.03194**, with nine of ten rounds faster. One ratio was 0.93815.
This is positive source-fixture evidence; the old arbitrary 5% rule does not
establish zero benefit. Assuming independent round observations, an exploratory
one-sided sign test is 0.01074, and a 97.85% distribution-free interval for the
median ratio is [1.01461, 1.08292]. These assumptions and one workload limit
generalization; none of these numbers is model tokens/s.

There is no new production adoption from this fixture. Production currently
submits pairs; realizing six-demand refill needs an actual multi-bank request
and readiness/lease integration, with its initialization, ownership and full
model costs included. The implemented fixture does not supply that connection.
